01 · The logo

An act marker, not a badge

The mark is a Roman numeral II, the second act, framed by an arch like the chapter opener of a well-made book or a programme page. The stacked lockup is the primary logo. Use the horizontal lockup where height is tight, and the monogram where the name already appears nearby.

02 · Space and size

Give it room to breathe

Call the height of the II inside the arch x. Keep at least ½x of clear space on every side of any lockup, free of text, edges and imagery. The dashed line shows this zone, and every exported file already includes it.

Minimum sizes
  • Monogram: at least 24 px tall on screen, 8 mm in print. Below 32 px, use the solid favicon version, whose II is cut out of a filled arch.
  • Full lockup: at least 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print. At that size the name reads clearly and ADVISORY is small but legible. Where you can, use 160 px or more.
  • Print: use the PDF or SVG files so edges stay sharp at any size.

CMYK values are straight conversions and only a starting point. Ask your printer for a proof before a large run. Contrast: burgundy and cream 9.78:1, burgundy on blush 9.31:1, dusty rose on cream 6.03:1, ink on cream 15.26:1. All pass WCAG AA for text. Primary colours are burgundy and cream. Use Dusty Rose for small accents and labels, and Warm Blush for light panels. No gradients, foil, shadows or bright red.

Playfair Display Italic
Headlines and the wordmark · weight 500

A high-contrast serif with a warm italic for emphasis, the same voice as your carousel headlines. Use italic for one idea per line, never whole paragraphs.

MONTSERRAT CAPS
Labels and descriptors · weight 500 · tracking 0.2–0.42em

Tracked uppercase for labels, step names and the ADVISORY descriptor. Body copy can use Montserrat at normal spacing, weight 400.

Licences: both families are published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. You can use them commercially in print, web, video and logos, and embed them in documents. The one restriction is that you can't sell the font files on their own. The licence texts are in the fonts/ folder. The logo files contain outlines, not fonts, so they need no font installed.

04 · Using the logo well

Do's and don'ts

DoBurgundy on cream or blush, with generous space.
DoReverse it out: the cream-on-burgundy file, or the white file on ink.
DoUse the one-colour ink version for single-colour print, forms and invoices.
DoLet the monogram stand alone once the name is already clear.
Don'tStretch, squash or skew. Scale by the corners only.
Don'tRecolour it. Only the five colourways supplied.
Don'tAdd shadows, glows, bevels, foil or outlines.
Don'tPlace it on busy photos or patterns. Use a flat panel of cream, blush or burgundy.

Also avoid rotating the logo, rearranging its parts, retyping the name in another font, or placing the II inside any shape other than its arch. For the Already Qualified podcast, use its own lockup rather than combining it with the main logo by hand.
